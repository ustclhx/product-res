---

Content lives in `apps/web/content`, one folder per collection and language:

- `blog/<locale>/<slug>.mdx` for blog posts
- `legal/<locale>/<page>.mdx` for the privacy policy, terms and refund policy
- `docs/<locale>/<slug>.mdx` for this documentation

Pages are rendered at build time, so they stay available even when the database is not.
