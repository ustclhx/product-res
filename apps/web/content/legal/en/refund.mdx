## No automatic refunds

Payments for subscriptions and credit packs are **not refunded by default**. This includes:

- **Cancelling a subscription**: the cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing
  period. You keep your plan and credits until then, and no partial refund is issued for the
  remaining time.
- **Deleting your account**: deleting your account cancels any active subscription and removes the
  remaining credits, without a refund.

## Requesting a refund

If you believe a refund is justified, you can submit a refund request:

1. Sign in and open the **Billing** page of your workspace, then choose **Request refund**. The
   account deletion flow also reminds you to request a refund before you delete your account.
2. Select the payment. Only successful payments made within the last **{{refundWindowDays}} days**
   can be selected, and each payment can have one open request at a time.
3. Explain the reason and leave a contact email so we can reach you even if you delete your account
   afterwards.

If you cannot sign in, email {{supportEmail}} with the payment details.

## Review

Every request is reviewed by our team. We may approve a full or partial refund, or decline the
request with a reason. You will receive an email with the decision either way.

## After a refund

The refund is returned through the original payment method. Once the payment provider confirms it,
the **remaining** credits granted by that payment are removed from your workspace; credits you have
already used are not charged back. Refunding a yearly plan removes the remaining credits of all its
monthly grants.

A payment dispute (chargeback) is handled the same way as a refund and may restrict the workspace
until it is resolved.
