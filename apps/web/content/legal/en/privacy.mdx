## Data we collect

- **Account data**: your name, email address and sign-in methods.
- **Billing data**: subscription and payment records. Card and wallet details are handled by our
  payment providers and never stored by {{appName}}.
- **Usage data**: the content you send to AI features and the credits consumed, used to provide the
  service and to bill you correctly.

## How we use it

We use your data to run the service, process payments, prevent abuse and contact you about your
account. We do not sell personal data.

## Your choices

You can update your profile in account settings and delete your account at any time. Deleting your
account removes or anonymises your personal data, except for records we must keep by law.
