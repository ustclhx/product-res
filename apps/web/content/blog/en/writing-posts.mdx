---
title: Writing posts with MDX
summary: Frontmatter fields, drafts and scheduled publishing.
date: 2026-09-20
tags: [guide]
---

Every post starts with frontmatter:

```yaml
title: My post
summary: One sentence shown in the list
date: 2026-10-01
tags: [guide]
draft: false
```

- **Drafts** (`draft: true`) are visible in local development and are left out of production builds.
- **Scheduled posts** use a `date` in the future and stay hidden until that time; lists are
  pre-rendered, so they show the post after the next deployment.
- **Translations** are optional: if a post has no version in the current language, the default
  language version is shown.
