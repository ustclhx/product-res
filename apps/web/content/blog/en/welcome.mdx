This blog is generated from MDX files in `apps/web/content/blog`. Each post lives in a folder
named after its language, and the file name becomes the address of the post.

## What is included

- Accounts, workspaces and team invitations
- Subscriptions, credit packs and a credit ledger
- AI text, image and video features with usage-based billing

## Making it yours

Replace the copy in `apps/web/messages`, the posts in `apps/web/content` and the theme variables
in `packages/ui`. Everything else keeps working.
